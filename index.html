Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how the Adam Is Bored Forum (forum.adamisbored.xyz) handles information when you use the forum.

Information we process

We use Firebase Authentication for account sign-in. Account information may include your email address and a unique account ID. We also store forum usernames, profile details (such as your bio and avatar), posts, replies, direct messages, and moderation information such as account bans.

Your password is handled by the authentication provider; the forum operator does not receive your plaintext password.

How information is used

We use this information to provide accounts, show public forum content, deliver direct messages, prevent abuse, apply rate limits, and enforce forum rules. Forum data is handled by our server-side API and stored using Google Firebase/Firestore. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, timestamps, and browser details, in server logs for operation and security.

Who can see what

Your username, public profile, posts, and replies can be seen by forum visitors. Direct messages are intended for their participants, but may be accessed when reasonably necessary to operate the service, investigate abuse, or comply with legal obligations. Do not post sensitive personal information.

Storage, retention, and your choices

Information is kept as needed to run and secure the forum. Deleting a post does not necessarily remove copies already seen or saved by others. If you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact adameschr@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your account, but never send your password. Some information may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy as the forum changes. The updated date above will show when it was last revised.

Terms of Use

Last updated: October 22, 2026

By using the Adam Is Bored Forum, you agree to follow these rules:

  • Treat others respectfully. Do not intimidate, mistreat, impersonate, or deliberately target other users.
  • Do not share content that breaks the law, promotes hostility toward others, is unsuitable for the forum, sends unwanted repeated messages, deceives users, or causes harm.
  • Respect everyone's privacy. Do not share another person's private information without permission.
  • Follow moderation decisions and technical limits. Do not try to get around content filters, account restrictions, request limits, or security measures.
  • Only access accounts, conversations, administrator features, and server systems that you are authorized to use.
  • Use suitable usernames and profile pictures. Do not share links or software intended to damage devices, accounts, or the service.

Moderation and availability

Posts, replies, profiles, or accounts may be limited or removed if these rules are broken. Automated moderation may sometimes make mistakes; contact us if you think a decision was incorrect. Forum features may change, become temporarily unavailable, or be discontinued.

Your content

You are responsible for content you submit. You should only upload material you have permission to share. By posting public content, you allow the forum to display it to visitors as part of the service.

Contact

For privacy questions, account requests, or reports of rule violations, email adameschr@gmail.com.
